## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: Luminous technical status telemetry pills, dark obsidian cards with subtle indigo hairline borders (`border-slate-800`), and four distinct evidence metric cards.
- Material & depth: Layered midnight surfaces (#0B0F19 background, #111827 card background, #1E293B muted controls) with 1px hairline dividers and glowing status badges.
- Containers & buttons: Crisp rounded-xl containers with high-contrast metric figures (Total Similarity, Plagiarism Risk Level, Uncited Direct Matches, Properly Cited & Attributed). Primary action buttons with solid violet-blue fill (#4F46E5 to #6366F1), dark pill toggles with active white badges.
- Layout rhythm: Alternating rich visual evidence sections (`Text | Screenshot` and `Screenshot | Text`), spacious centered hero workspace with above-the-fold live checker dominance.

## Animation
- Entrance: Subtle fade-in on scan completion (200ms ease-out)
- Interaction: Micro-scale hover feedback on evidence tabs and action buttons (150ms ease)
- Scroll / transition: Smooth scroll focus to live workspace on CTA click
